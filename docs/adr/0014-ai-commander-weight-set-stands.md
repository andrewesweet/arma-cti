# The AI Commander's first weight set stands

Delegated-decision: yes
Date: 2026-07-31
Stood-in-for: gameplay balance/feel sign-off on the judgement calls flagged in #16 ("The weights are a gameplay-feel surface and want sign-off")
Reviewed-by-human: pending

The seeded utility scorer (`src/cti_daemon/planner.py`, `Weights`) shipped with judgement calls flagged for sign-off rather than assumed. The aggression retune ("press rather than hold", `00836cf`) was already a human decision on 2026-07-31; this ADR discharges the rest. Judged against the North Star — engaging tactical combat for squad-leader players, meaningful strategic control for Commander players, competent and challenging AI Commanders — all four stand unchanged. No code moves.

**`jitter < travel` (0.3 against 1.0) stands.** The seed may only break ties geography has left within ~300 m; it must never send a Squad the long way round. A Commander whose routes are legible from the map is one a player can out-think for real reasons, and the probe's first run showed exactly what violating this looks like: a 2,065 m march chosen over a 1,076 m one by 0.22 of flavour. Overturned only if seeded variety proves too weak to distinguish Campaigns — and the remedy would still be more tied geography, not a louder seed.

**`garrison` 0.1 against `income` 1.0 stands.** `Campaign._advance` keeps ground once taken, so quiet ground pays nothing extra for being stood on, and a Commander that garrisons cannot win by Domination — it would concede the strategic game outright, failing both the challenge and the contest. Which garrison matters is the `threat`/`defend` terms' job, and demonstrably works (WEST-4 below). Overturning evidence: #17's two-sided runs or a playtest showing held ground flipping back cheaply enough that raiding dominates and fronts never form.

**Cheapest-Squad purchase stands for the MVP, `weapons` never bought as its accepted consequence.** Capture is by presence, every Squad stands in one radius, so under MVP rules numbers strictly beat firepower; buying `weapons` without a threat model to justify it would be spending the AI's Funds on looking varied. The cost is real — squad-leader players only ever meet rifle squads — and is accepted, not overlooked. Overturning evidence: playtest reports of monotonous contacts, or any rules change (support radii, vehicle capture, threat-aware purchasing) that gives firepower a measurable return; at that point purchase needs a threat model, which is its own ticket, not a weight tweak.

**The `UNKNOWN_THREAT`-driven garrison stands.** Held ground nobody watches scores as possibly holding a team, so the probe's WEST-4 was bought and set to defend Agia Marina unprompted. That is rear security falling out of the fog rule rather than a scripted behaviour: unknown is not empty, for our ground as much as theirs, and a Commander that leaves its rear open is not a competent one. It also gives squad-leader players garrisons to fight through rather than a conga line of empty towns. Overturning evidence: unattended runs where garrisoning accretes until the advance stalls short of Domination — the fix would be decaying the floor on own held ground, not zeroing it.
