### Changed

- **Four seats carry new profile preferences, and `review` no longer borrows the implementer's (human ruling 2026-08-27, ADR-0071 Amendment A10).** `implementer` prefers `zai-glm53flash-max`, `codex-luna-max`, `opus-low` and escalates through `codex-sol-high`, `zai-glm53-max`, `opus-high`; `recon` prefers `zai-glm53flash-high`, `codex-luna-medium`, `haiku-medium`; `retro` prefers `fable-high`, `opus-xhigh`, `codex-sol-max` and escalates through `fable-xhigh`, `opus-max` where its entry was `opus-max`, `fable-max`; and `review` carries its own `codex-sol-xhigh`, `zai-glm53-max`, `opus-medium` with its own `codex-sol-max`, `opus-xhigh` escalation entry, where the registry had shared one tuple between the two seats so they could not drift apart. Nothing about how a review resolves changed: the profile under review and every profile the issue's records place on the work are still removed, a different lane is still preferred rather than required, and `review_same_profile` is still absolute. Three consequences are worth stating: the implementer's head now sits on the lane the human's rule of 2026-08-05 confines to off-peak hours, so a dispatch inside z.ai's published peak band resolves past it and records that it did — reaching `codex-luna-max` where that profile is itself dispatchable, and walking on down the list where it is not; `review`'s first `claude-native` entry moved from `opus-low` to `opus-medium`; and `retro`'s arbiter is now `fable-xhigh` where its preference head is `fable-high`, so the arbiter and the profile most likely to have authored the rounds it judges share a model, which the cell this replaces was filled to avoid.

### Added

- **GLM-5.3-Flash is registered on the z.ai lane as `zai-glm53flash-max` and `zai-glm53flash-high`.** The lane's sonnet slot, which had been pointing at `glm-5.3` as a synonym for the opus slot, now names `glm-5.3-flash`, so the third slot is a third arm rather than a spare name and no existing profile lost its model. The slug was read back from the endpoint's own model list on the day of the ruling — `GET /api/paas/v4/models` returns ten models, `glm-5.3-flash` among them — rather than taken from z.ai's Claude Code integration page, which documents no default Claude-name mapping for it. What the two Flash names are worth against each other is unmeasured, exactly as the registry already records for `zai-glm53-max` against `zai-glm53-high`: they differ in `output_config.effort` in the request body, and z.ai's handling of that field has not been measured on this lane.
