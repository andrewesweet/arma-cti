### Added

- **The observatory's rework view: the ruled ranking key, with dispatches per issue beside it (#487).** `just observatory` now reads the review journal (`~/.arma-cti/review/`, one `loop.json` per issue) as a third source and ships two new tables. `profile_rework` holds one row per (profile, seat) with ADR-0071 ruling 6's key — fix rounds per landing — computed for implementer-seat profiles and no others; the ranked seat set is derived from the seat registries (`dispatch.SEATS`' `lands` column crossed with `ledger`'s `seat_shape`) and never named as a list. A profile with no landings keeps its rounds visible and its rate undefined, never a division; the `landings` denominator counts a dispatch whenever its issue landed while it was open, not that the dispatch produced the landing — an over-inclusive bound stated in the schema reference, with the semantics fix filed as #542. A seat that lands nothing by contract reports its rework unranked with a reason naming the contract, and the retro seat's journal landings are excluded by the same derivation. `issue_rework` holds dispatches per issue — the unranked companion, `ranked` `0` on every row. The stratification is pre-work only (the dispatch record's own `profile` and `seat`), the outcome columns carry a `measures` marker naming them description in the output itself, and the rebuild's new `rework` line states the key's own spread (`round_zero` against `loops`, `key_varies`) and marks its sample limit an estimate rather than a measurement. The store's schema moves to `cti.observatory/3`; a store of any other schema still refuses at open.
